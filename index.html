Course Description

This programme develops professional knowledge and practical competencies in modern Human Resource Management. It covers strategic HRM, talent acquisition, performance management, employment law, compensation, employee engagement, HR analytics and digital HR practices. The programme prepares participants to undertake HR responsibilities and contribute effectively to organisational performance.

Is this diploma right for you?

Individuals who have completed their A/L examinations, holders of other academic or professional qualifications, aspiring HR professionals, administrative officers, supervisors and working professionals with relevant industry experience who wish to develop professional competencies in Human Resource Management.

Course Objectives

  • Develop an understanding of strategic Human Resource Management.
  • Apply professional recruitment and talent acquisition practices.
  • Develop workforce planning and talent management strategies.
  • Manage performance appraisal and employee development processes.
  • Understand compensation, benefits and payroll management.
  • Interpret and apply relevant employment legislation and HR compliance requirements.
  • Manage employee relations, grievances and disciplinary procedures.
  • Apply HR analytics, HRIS and digital HR technologies.
  • Develop leadership, decision-making and organisational management skills.
  • Apply professional HR knowledge through practical projects and case studies.

Program Outline / Modules

  1. 01Strategic Human Resource Management

    Connect people priorities to business goals and explore HR’s role in organisational strategy.

  2. 02Talent Acquisition and Workforce Planning

    Explore how organisations plan staffing needs, attract candidates and make selection decisions.

  3. 03Learning and Development Management

    Identify learning needs and consider how to plan and evaluate staff development.

  4. 04Performance and Talent Management

    Explore how to set expectations, give feedback and support progression.

  5. 05Compensation, Benefits and Payroll Management

    Understand the parts of a reward package and the role of payroll in HR operations.

  6. 06Employment Law and Industrial Relations

    Explore the role of employment rules, workplace policies and industrial relations in HR.

  7. 07Employee Engagement and Relationship Management

    Consider how communication, feedback and workplace relationships affect engagement.

  8. 08Organisational Behaviour and Development

    Examine how individuals and teams behave at work and how organisations manage change.

  9. 09HR Analytics and Digital Human Resource Management

    Explore how people data and digital tools can inform HR decisions.

  10. 10Leadership and Strategic Management

    Connect leadership choices to organisational priorities and team outcomes.

  11. 11HR Policies, Administration and Professional Practices

    Study how consistent policies and professional practice support everyday HR work.

  12. 12HR Project and Practical Application

    Bring the modules together by applying HR concepts to a practical project.
