01 / THE PROGRAMME

Build a career
around people.

For aspiring HR professionals, business partners and anyone ready to take the next step in HR. You’ll study how organisations recruit, develop and retain people, and how HR supports decisions across the business.

Talent acquisitionPeople developmentHR strategy

02 / CURRICULUM

What you’ll learn.

Explore 12 modules, from workforce planning and employment relations to a practical HR project.

Ask about the course ↗
01Strategic Human Resource Management

Connect people priorities to business goals and explore HR’s role in organisational strategy.

02Talent Acquisition and Workforce Planning

Explore how organisations plan staffing needs, attract candidates and make selection decisions.

03Learning and Development Management

Identify learning needs and consider how to plan and evaluate staff development.

04Performance and Talent Management

Explore how to set expectations, give feedback and support progression.

05Compensation, Benefits and Payroll Management

Understand the parts of a reward package and the role of payroll in HR operations.

06Employment Law and Industrial Relations

Explore the role of employment rules, workplace policies and industrial relations in HR.

07Employee Engagement and Relationship Management

Consider how communication, feedback and workplace relationships affect engagement.

08Organisational Behaviour and Development

Examine how individuals and teams behave at work and how organisations manage change.

09HR Analytics and Digital Human Resource Management

Explore how people data and digital tools can inform HR decisions.

10Leadership and Strategic Management

Connect leadership choices to organisational priorities and team outcomes.

11HR Policies, Administration and Professional Practices

Study how consistent policies and professional practice support everyday HR work.

12HR Project and Practical Application

Bring the modules together by applying HR concepts to a practical project.
